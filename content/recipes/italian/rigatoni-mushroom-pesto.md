## Ingredients

*Serves 2*

- 250g rigatoni
- 250g brown mushrooms
- 150g baby spinach
- ½ red onion, finely diced (brunoise)
- 200ml double cream
- 3 tbsp pesto
- 30g Parmesan, grated
- 50ml olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and chilli flakes

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil.

2. Sauté the onion in olive oil over medium heat until softened. Add the mushrooms and cook until lightly browned.

3. Cook the rigatoni for 8 minutes until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.

4. Add the cream to the mushroom pan and reduce by a third. Season with salt and pepper, then stir in the pesto on low heat.

5. Add the drained pasta to the sauce with 2 tbsp of pasta water and the spinach. Toss until the spinach wilts.

6. Serve topped with Parmesan and chilli flakes.

## Notes

The pasta water is the key step — the starch thickens the sauce and rounds out the flavour. Don't skip it.
