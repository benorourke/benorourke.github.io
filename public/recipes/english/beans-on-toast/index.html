Proper Beans on Toast

Posted on 22 Mar 2026

Ingredients

  • 1 tin Heinz baked beans
  • 2 thick slices white bread (Warburtons or similar)
  • Salted butter
  • Optional: a splash of Worcestershire sauce, pinch of smoked paprika

Method

  1. Toast the bread. While still hot, butter generously — the butter should melt into the bread completely.

  2. Heat the beans in a small saucepan on medium-low. Stir occasionally. If using Worcestershire sauce, add it now.

  3. When the beans are steaming (not boiling — boiling makes them watery), pour over the toast.

  4. Dust with smoked paprika if you like.

Notes

The ratio is important. One tin between two slices is the correct amount. Any less and it’s dry. Any more and the toast goes soggy before you finish.
