Butter Chicken

Posted on 10 Apr 2026

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 700g chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 150g full-fat yoghurt
  • 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt

Sauce

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 medium onions, roughly chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 150ml double cream
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix the marinade ingredients with the chicken and leave for at least 2 hours (overnight is better). Grill or cook in a hot dry pan until charred at the edges. Set aside.

  2. Fry the onions in butter and oil on medium heat until deeply golden — about 20 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 more minutes.

  3. Add tomatoes and spices, simmer 15 minutes. Blend smooth. Pass through a sieve if you want restaurant-level texture.

  4. Return to the pan, add the chicken and cream. Simmer 10 minutes. Adjust salt and add sugar to balance the tomato acidity.

  5. Serve with basmati rice or naan.

Notes

The char on the chicken matters — it gives the sauce some depth. Don’t skip it.
