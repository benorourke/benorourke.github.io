Chicken Bhuna

Posted on 5 Jul 2026

Ingredients

Serves 3

  • 3 chicken fillets, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 5 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp hing (asafoetida)
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 fresh green chilli, finely chopped
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp natural yoghurt, whisked
  • Fresh coriander, to garnish

Spice paste

  • ⅓ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil, to loosen

Method

  1. Prep everything first. Mix the turmeric, cumin powder, coriander powder, chilli powder, salt, and garam masala with oil until the consistency of yoghurt. Have all other ingredients ready before you start cooking.

  2. Vaghar (tempering). Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, hing, and dried red chilli and let them sizzle for 30 seconds.

  3. Fry the onion. Add the onion and fry until soft and golden.

  4. Add ginger, garlic, and chilli. Fry for 1 minute.

  5. Check the oil. You should see a little oil floating in the onion mixture — add more if needed. This matters for cooking the spice paste properly.

  6. Cook the spice paste. Turn the heat to high, add the paste, and stir constantly so it doesn’t stick. Cook until the oil begins to separate from the spices.

  7. Seal the chicken. Add the chicken and stir-fry on high heat to seal it and keep the juices in.

  8. Add the tomatoes. Once the chicken is mostly cooked through (cut a piece to check), add the chopped tomatoes a little at a time, keeping the heat high.

  9. Simmer. Once all the tomatoes are in, lower the heat, cover, and simmer until the chicken is cooked through.

  10. Taste and adjust spices as needed.

  11. Stir in the yoghurt. Turn the heat to low and add the yoghurt one tablespoon at a time, stirring constantly to prevent curdling.

  12. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve.
